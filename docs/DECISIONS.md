Details: docs/llm-provider.md | OpenAI(-compatible) chat/completions with a key; in-browser transformers.js model as the live provider; Workers AI models without top_logprobs (e.g. qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8 only has prompt_logprobs) | CLAUDE.md prefers Workers AI if it returns logprobs: the published schemas of glm-4.7-flash and about 11 other Workers AI models include `top_logprobs` (0-20) with an OpenAI-shaped response.
