What is a good first hike near a lake for a beginner?


Pick a loop under 8 km with less than 300 m of climbing, and start early so you have shade and parking.

Bring water, a snack, a light layer and a paper map.


Thanks. What if it rains?


Check the forecast the evening before. Light rain is fine with a shell jacket; skip the hike if thunderstorms are forecast.
